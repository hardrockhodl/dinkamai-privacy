DinKamai Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

DinKamai is an offline app that helps gig workers see what they really earned each day. It is built so that your information stays on your phone.

What the app stores

For each day you save, DinKamai stores three things on your device: the date, the amount you earned and the amount you spent. Nothing else.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, send or share any data. The app has no internet permission and cannot connect to the internet.
  • It has no account, login or sign-up, and asks for no personal information.
  • It contains no ads, analytics or tracking.
  • It does not read your SMS, contacts, location, bank or payment apps.
  • Android cloud backup is turned off for the app, so your entries are not uploaded to any cloud.

Deleting your data

You can delete any day inside the app. Uninstalling DinKamai, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes all of its data from your phone.

Purchases

DinKamai is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google under Google's privacy policy; the app never sees your payment details.

Children

DinKamai is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
